1. Statistics and Probability Basics

Mean (expected value)¶

The value a random variable takes on average. For returns, it is the long-run average return.

The Return Distribution: Mean and Volatility

Volatility¶

The standard deviation of returns. A larger value means returns spread more widely around the mean.

Tax shield¶

The tax saved because interest expense lowers taxable income. It grows with debt, while bankruptcy costs push the other way.
