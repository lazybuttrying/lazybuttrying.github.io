## <span style="color:#7c3aed; font-weight:800;">Passions</span>

I’m motivated by one question: how can data and intelligent systems improve <span style="font-weight:700; color:#7c3aed;">high-stakes decisions</span> without sacrificing <span style="font-style:italic; text-decoration:underline;">accountability and fairness</span>? My interests sit at the intersection of finance, computer science, and social computing, where technical choices shape market outcomes, institutional behavior, and who benefits from innovation.

Across my research, I build evidence and tools that make complex systems more transparent and measurable. I’ve worked on projects spanning market data infrastructure and empirical measurement (e.g., constructing an alternative index for the Korean bond market using issuance and trading data), governance and incentives in decentralized systems (e.g., analyzing DAO voting behavior and outcomes), and applied machine learning for organizational decision contexts (e.g., developing diagnostic visualizations to understand shared representations in deep-learning models used for corporate M&A settings).

I also care about how technologies land in real institutions. Through my work improving accessibility in an academic administration portal for students with disabilities, I saw how “small” design decisions can systematically include—or exclude—people. That experience strengthened my commitment to research that connects rigorous methods to real-world constraints: building systems people can actually trust, govern, and use.

Going forward, I want to develop research that is both technically grounded and socially informed: combining careful measurement, computational methods, and institutional understanding to produce insights that matter for practice and policy.

## <span style="color:#7c3aed; font-weight:800;">Positions</span>

I am an M.S. student in Finance (Business Administration) at Seoul National University (Mar 2024–present). Alongside my graduate coursework, I conduct interdisciplinary research bridging finance and machine learning, including (i) constructing an alternative index for the Korean bond market using issuance and secondary trading data (AXI project), (ii) empirical analysis of DAO governance and voting outcomes, and (iii) developing diagnostic visualizations for deep-learning models used in corporate M&A decision settings (with Prof. Jongsub Lee and Hayong Yun).

Before this, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering from Ewha Womans University (Magna Cum Laude; Mar 2018–Aug 2023). I’ve also gained applied experience across research and industry, including an internship at NEXTLab (intelligent automation; deploying detection models on-device with TensorFlow Lite/OpenCV), and project work spanning end-to-end ML systems (MLOps, full-stack engineering, and computer vision). In parallel, I served as a Junior Ombudsperson (BK21-supported) on a project improving the accessibility of an academic administration portal for students with disabilities—strengthening my commitment to human-centered, real-world deployment of technology.
